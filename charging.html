Tesla Model Y Playbook

safety & charging

Charging & range

Drive by the in-car Trip Planner: it routes you through Superchargers and warms the battery for fast charging before arrival. Back it up with A Better Routeplanner (ABRP) and PlugShare for live status. Charge to 100% the night before each big day and aim to reach each Supercharger with about 20% left. Loaded with five people, climbing mountains, and running AC, real range runs well under the dash estimate, so plan conservatively.

Beating the desert heat

Each morning while still plugged in, use Scheduled Departure to pre-cool the cabin on grid power, not the battery, so you leave full and cold. Keep Cabin Overheat Protection on when parked. Driving, keep windows up and climate on Auto. At stops, park and charge in shade; in extreme heat the car cools its battery before it will charge fast.

Learn the car first

It's a rental, so spend 20 minutes in a parking lot: door handles, drive/reverse, one-pedal regen braking, opening the charge port, plugging into a Supercharger. Set up phone keys for each driver and update the software before departure. Treat Autopilot as assistance only, hands on the wheel. Find the tire repair kit. There is no spare.

On-road safety

Swap drivers every 2-3 hours and never push through drowsiness. Seatbelts for all five, every leg. Carry more water than feels necessary (the desert is unforgiving if you break down), plus a first-aid kit, flashlight, and snacks. August is monsoon season in New Mexico and Arizona: watch for afternoon storms and never drive through a flooded wash. Download offline maps for dead-signal stretches.

Build charging into the schedule

Supercharger stops run 20-40 minutes versus five for gas, so fold them into meals and leg-stretches. It nudges arrival times later, especially on the long return days.
