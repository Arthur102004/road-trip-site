Outbound Thu, Jul 30

Land in OKC

The two early-arriving drivers land in Oklahoma City, pick up the Model Y, and grab supplies. Arthur doesn't land until tomorrow morning. See the note below.

  • Tonight: plug in and charge to 100% overnight; spend 20 minutes learning the car.
Eat Wingstop · Cattlemen's Steakhouse (historic Stockyards) · Tucker's Onion Burgers · Florence's · dinner and a walk around Bricktown.
💡 Suggested plan: since only two of the three drivers are here today, use tonight to get the Model Y fully ready (100% charge, supplies loaded, controls learned) so it's sitting done and charged when Arthur lands tomorrow morning. That way nobody has to sit around waiting on car prep once he's off the plane.
Outbound Fri, Jul 31

OKC → Santa Rosa, NM

~430 mi wake 9:00
⚠️ Tight morning, confirmed: Arthur lands OKC at 10:14 AM (Delta 1108 → Delta 2490), the same day the group rolls out on this 430-mile drive. Recommended: with the car already prepped and charged from last night, the other two drivers pick Arthur up straight from the airport and the group heads out from there. No need to push the whole day's departure to the afternoon and eat into driving daylight. If the airport pickup runs long, sliding departure to early afternoon is the fallback.
  • Roll out west on I-40.
  • Cadillac Ranch in Amarillo (free, bring spray paint).
  • Arrive Santa Rosa; visit the Route 66 Auto Museum (~$5).
⚡ Charge Leave OKC at 100%; Supercharge in Amarillo while at Cadillac Ranch; top up in Tucumcari if needed.
Eat Wingstop (OKC/Amarillo) · The Big Texan (Amarillo) · Joseph's Bar & Grill (Santa Rosa).
Outbound Sat, Aug 1

UWC Montezuma → Albuquerque

~185 mi wake 9:00
  • North from Santa Rosa to Las Vegas, NM (~75 mi).
  • UWC-USA, Montezuma: campus, grounds, and the free Montezuma Hot Springs (shallow sit-and-soak, no swimming needed). Take your time since it's a short drive today.
  • South to Albuquerque for the night.
⚡ Charge Short day; top up in Las Vegas, NM if needed, then charge overnight in Albuquerque.
Eat Wingstop · Frontier (UNM icon, cheap) · El Pinto · Sadie's · Green Jeans Food Hall. Order chile "Christmas" (red + green).
Book ahead: Aug 1 is a Saturday and UWC is closed weekends. Email admissions this week to arrange a Saturday visit.
Outbound Sun, Aug 2

Albuquerque → Phoenix

~465 mi wake 9:00
  • I-40 west to Flagstaff (cool pines, coffee), then I-17 south.
  • Phoenix heat: early August runs 110-115°F. Treat daytime as indoor AC time (aquarium, arcade, mall, movie); explore after sunset (Roosevelt Row, Mill Avenue in Tempe, or an air-conditioned D-backs game at Chase Field).
⚡ Charge Longest desert day, plan two stops. Leave ABQ full; Superchargers at Gallup, then Flagstaff or Camp Verde. Loaded and hot, real range runs well under the dash number.
Eat Wingstop · Pizzeria Bianco · Little Miss BBQ.
Outbound → Vegas Mon, Aug 3

Phoenix → Hoover Dam → Las Vegas

~300 mi wake 9:00
  • Northwest on US-93 through Wickenburg and Kingman.
  • Hoover Dam (walk across, ~1 hr, $10 parking).
  • Arrive Las Vegas, meet the two friends flying in today, check into the booked Airbnb ("Modern 5B/3B, Near Strip").
⚡ Charge Leave Phoenix full; Supercharge in Kingman before the quiet US-93 stretch.
Getting around Vegas: the Model Y fits all 5 for day trips (Lee Canyon, groceries, airport). For Strip nights, an Uber XL / Lyft XL also seats 5 or 6 and skips parking.

Grand Junction → Hays, KS

~530 mi wake 9:00
  • I-70 east through the Rockies (Glenwood Canyon, Vail Pass), then down through Denver and east into Kansas.
